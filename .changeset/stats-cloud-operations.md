The private stats view shows how FTW Cloud runs: boxes and phones connected, the share on QUIC, data out this month against the free allowance, drops, push results, assistant calls, latency and each deploy's drain, from hourly sums over the whole service. It also shows the exact fleet and energy report totals for closed days. Nothing in it names a home, a device or an address.
