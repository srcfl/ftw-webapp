Now draws your home as the phone app does: what the home uses sits in the middle of a ring, and the sun, the battery, the grid and the car sit in the corners as plain text. Each one lights the part of the ring that faces it, longer with more power, and a soft light runs along it toward the home or away from it. Only readings the box confirms live glow or move; old readings show as still grey arcs. Tap a corner or the middle to open it, as before. The first load is about 5 kB smaller.
