The app now uses the phone app's colours. The grid is one pink colour both ways, and the sign and the word say whether you buy or sell. The battery is cyan when it stores power and orange when it gives power, and the car is violet. Green now only means live or confirmed, and red only means refused, failed or blocked. Prices and savings are shades of ink, not red and green.
