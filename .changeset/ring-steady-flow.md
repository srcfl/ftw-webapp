Now shows which way your power flows, as the phone app does: thin lines of light run between each part of the ring and the home in the middle, in toward the home for power coming into it and out for power leaving it, faster with more power. Each new reading from your box sends one soft swell along the arcs that are live. With reduced motion turned on, the lines hold still and a small arrow shows the direction instead.
